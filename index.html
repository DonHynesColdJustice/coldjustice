The Story
CJ

A true-to-life crime thriller drawn from years on the streets of Detroit.

Detroit Police Officer Eddie Ferguson plays by his own rules, but the FBI and the federal court system have an agenda of their own: to win at all costs.

Rookie Donnie Dent learns this the hard way on his first night in a squad car. A harsh, unwritten code forces him to choose between loyalty to his badge and a chilling truth that could get him killed.

"In a city where everyone is a suspect and even the law itself can't be trusted, who is the hunter, and who is the hunted?"

Meanwhile, Nurse Sarah Sanders, plagued by a family tragedy, becomes convinced that Ferguson is responsible — and she'll stop at nothing to expose the conspiracy. As layers of deceit peel back, revealing shocking betrayals and a meticulously planned frame-up, the true cost of justice becomes horrifyingly clear.
